## 📖 Personalized Lesson Sections

### 1. Step 1: The Space Docking Problem (Why Units Must Match)
> 💡 **High-Interest Analogy:** *Space station fuel docking*
> ♿ **Accommodations Applied:** visual_scaffolding, high_interest_analogy, chunked_instructions

Imagine Rocket A carries 1/2 tank of fuel and Rocket B carries 1/4 tank. We cannot simply add the tank parts together directly because the containers are cut into different sized pieces! To combine them, we need both containers partitioned into the same size compartments (fourths).

**Check for Understanding:** Why can't we add 1/2 and 1/4 by just adding 1+1 on top and 2+4 on bottom?

### 2. Step 2: Scaling Fractions with Visual Fraction Bars
> 💡 **High-Interest Analogy:** *Fraction length bars*
> ♿ **Accommodations Applied:** visual_scaffolding, chunked_instructions

Look at the visual fraction bar: 1/2 is the exact same length as 2/4. When we replace 1/2 with 2/4, our math problem becomes 2/4 + 1/4. Now that both parts have the common denominator 4, we simply add the numerators: 2 fourths + 1 fourth = 3 fourths (3/4).

**Check for Understanding:** What equivalent fraction with denominator 4 has the same value as 1/2?


## ✏️ Scaffolded Practice Exercises

#### Problem 1 (Scaffolded Tier)
**Problem:** Using a fraction bar model, find 1/3 + 1/6.

<details><summary>🔍 Socratic Hints & Solution</summary>

- **Tier 1 (Guiding Question):** Can 1/3 be sliced into equal sixths?
- **Tier 2 (Strategy Clue):** Multiply both numerator and denominator of 1/3 by 2 to find equivalent sixths.
- **Tier 3 (Worked Sub-Step):** 1/3 = 2/6. Now compute 2/6 + 1/6.
- **Final Solution Key:** `3/6 (or 1/2 simplified)`
</details>

#### Problem 2 (Standard Tier)
**Problem:** Solve: 2/5 + 3/10

<details><summary>🔍 Socratic Hints & Solution</summary>

- **Tier 1 (Guiding Question):** What is the common denominator between 5 and 10?
- **Tier 2 (Strategy Clue):** Convert 2/5 into tenths first.
- **Tier 3 (Worked Sub-Step):** 2/5 * (2/2) = 4/10. Now add 4/10 + 3/10.
- **Final Solution Key:** `7/10`
</details>
